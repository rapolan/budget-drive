# UX Notes (Session D)

Subjective observations from a Playwright smoke-run of the manual test checklist. These are not bugs and were not fixed — collected here for human review only.

- **Dashboard "today's schedule" widget is date-relative to seed time, not test time.** The seed files (`002_demo_lessons_payments.sql`, `003_manual_test_dataset.sql`) generate lesson dates using `CURRENT_DATE` at the moment `run-seed.js` is executed. If you seed the database and then test hours or days later, the widget correctly shows nothing for "today" — the seeded lessons have scrolled into the past. This is expected behavior, not broken, but it means the checklist's "today's schedule" verification step is only meaningful immediately after seeding. Worth deciding whether the seed should generate dates relative to the lesson list's likely testing window instead of seed time, or whether the checklist should just say "re-seed immediately before this specific check."
- **The invite-role dropdown doesn't offer `owner` or `viewer`**, even though both are valid roles in the `user_tenant_memberships` schema (`owner/admin/instructor/staff/viewer`). Only `admin`/`staff`/`instructor` are selectable from the Team Settings invite modal. This may be intentional (inviting a second owner or a read-only viewer might be deliberately rare/sensitive), but it's worth a product decision on whether those two roles should ever be reachable via the invite UI, or only via direct DB/role-change actions.
